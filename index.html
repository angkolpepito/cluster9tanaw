What the cluster office can see

Every figure is filtered by school or combined for the whole cluster.

Learners and enrolment

Enrolment for the previous and current school year, plus IP learners and CCT beneficiaries per grade level and gender.

Personnel and positions

Staff per department, the distribution of positions, and a personnel roster with birthdate, employment status, years in service and years since the last promotion.

Teacher performance in PMES

Teacher ratings from Outstanding to Poor for the last three school years, school by school.

Performance indicators

Simple dropout, repetition, graduation, completion and promotion rates, previous school year against current.

GSA and MPS

Pulled straight from each school’s Project COMPASS: per subject, per learning area, per exam.

MOOE and GAD

Allocation and utilization per quarter, with balance, percent used and the GAD allocation or attribution.

OK sa DepEd

BMI, Gulayan sa Paaralan, canteen, reproductive health and zero single-use plastic, school by school.

Project HANDA

Mock NAT results for Grades 10 and 12, previous against current school year, with school rankings and the top 10 learners in the Cluster Mock Test.

Reading, ARAL and operations

Phil-IRI reading levels, ARAL Basic and ARAL Plus progress, key school operations, SGC preparedness, PTA participation, land ownership and metal detectors.

Innovations and technical assistance

Each school’s best practices, and the Pakig-AMBIT, Project BISITA and PSDS monitoring received since SY 2024-2025.

ALS, SNED and SLAC

ALS Junior and Senior High School enrollees, SNED learners per school by grade level, category and gender, and School Learning Action Cell plans, approvals and sessions.

How it works

The PSDS office adds schools

Enter each school’s name and school ID, then issue its login.

School heads encode

Each head signs in and fills in only their own school. COMPASS sends scores automatically.

The PSDS monitors

Open the dashboard to compare schools, spot gaps and print reports.
